## What shipped in this batch

**The footer is a proper piece of work now** (`src/ui/Footer.tsx`, new). Three blocks:

1. *Nothing phones home* — the live count of requests to other servers, drawn at 42 px, counted by
   the visitor's own browser. It used to be an 11px grey sentence at the very bottom.
2. *This build* — version stamp, today's book seal, `online` / `in a browser tab`, and the
   status-strip clip.
3. *Made by* — giveabit.io, MIT, one file that still opens offline, and the install button.

Under it, the word `slip` in serif and the promise in words. Reading size throughout (16px); the old
footer was 11px. Three columns from 900px, stacked below, no horizontal overflow at 320px.
`RequestCounter` grew a `hero` variant so the big number and the small number can never disagree.
