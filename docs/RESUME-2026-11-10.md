## Correction, same day: the footer had to be rebuilt

v0.31.25 shipped a footer that ran edge to edge on a wide screen while the till above it sat in a
1152px column, and its closing line sat under the fixed charge bar (126px) and tab bar (65px) on a
phone. The owner's words: "does not fit at all". Rebuilt in v0.31.26:

- every measurement now comes from the page's own column (`main` is 1152px wide with 20px of inner
  padding), never from the viewport — the footer sits at 164→1276px, the same as `[data-panel="till"]`;
- two columns (proof | this build) and one closing line, instead of three columns of equal claim;
- extra bottom padding only on the till, where the phone's fixed bars exist. `data-section` on the
  shell root says which section is open, so Books does not get 216px of dead space;
- the giant zero is 32px inline, not 42px on its own.

The header also has its stencil back: `public/header-tex.svg`, a tiled brass pattern at 0.2 opacity,
drifting one 1600px tile every two minutes with a transform-only animation. Off-centre rings so they
never halo the wordmark; pixel-anchored masks because the element is a whole tile wider than the
screen. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not move.


## The counter read 1, not 0

Cloudflare injected `static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js` into every page on the
giveabit.io zone. Our CSP blocked it, so nothing ever left the browser (transferSize 0, request
failed) — but Chrome still logs the attempt, so the footer's own measurement read 1 against a
headline that says nothing phones home. Also: the raw 120-character URL used to run across both
footer columns.

- Fixed in the code: the counter names the **host**, never the URL (`src/core/net-count.ts`), and
  `tests/net-count.test.ts` pins the exact beacon URL that caused it.
- Fixed at the edge: `DELETE /accounts/{account_id}/rum/site_info/edd7637…` removed the giveabit.io
  Web Analytics site. `auto_install:false` (PUT) is accepted but does not stop injection.
- Restore if ever needed: `/root/.hermes/cache/scratch/restore/giveabit-rum-site-20261110-1321.json`.
- Verified live: 0 off-origin requests, counter reads 0, no overflow at 1440 or 390.

## The three money marks (v0.31.28 → v0.31.29)

Chosen from the design brief rather than the feature list: three things a shopkeeper does not ask
for, but that make the till look like it was made by hand.

1. **The brass rule** — one 2px line of the site's brass, 56px wide, under the single number a screen
   exists for: the pay amount, the receipt, the day's total on Books, the customer's screen. It was
   already on the charge bar as a hardcoded `#f0a32a` border, which is why the token gate never saw
   it; moved onto the token, the gate caught it, and the selector is now named in the allow-list.
2. **The engraved figure** — tabular numerals, −0.02em tracking, one hairline shadow in the light
   theme only. Dark gets none: a shadow on a night page smears the digits.
3. **The receipt as a document** — the status is the shared `Badge`, so slip-observed is a solid box,
   a manual confirmation is a dashed box, a rehearsal is hatched; the shape survives a black-and-white
   print. The words stay frozen.

The receipt's code was also broken and nobody had noticed: a fixed 128px white box held a 225px SVG,
so the code spilled past the card and lost its quiet zone. It is sized by its artwork now and was
decoded from the live screen to prove it (`bitcoin:bc1qcr8te4kr609g…`).

Then the phone: at 320 and 390 the card was 10–45px wider than itself, the words broke mid-word, and
the badge pushed past the screen edge. Under 520px the code and the words stack; under 420px the
badge drops under the amount; only machine strings may break mid-word. Measured after: 0 overflow at
320, 390, 768 and 1440.

Live proof on v0.31.29: amount `5,834 sats` visible, brass rule 12px beneath it and left-aligned on
phone and desk, one rule per screen, receipt card 0 overflow, no page errors.
