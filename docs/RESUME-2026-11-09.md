## Do not do these

- Do not deploy this commit and then say the camera works. `_headers` is served by Cloudflare, not by the Vite preview.
- Do not move `#tl-rate` back into the charge bar. The bar shows `.v2-rate-line`. The field stays in the open Tools drawer so the suites can still fill it.
- Do not put a space character inside a grouped address. Browser suites read `innerText` and expect the plain address. Gaps are margins on spans.
- Do not echo a scanned secret. `assertNotSecret` runs before the field is written, in both Money in and setup.
- Do not treat "Leave setup" as the old "skip to the workbench" string. The merchant guide and `docs/ONBOARDING-SPEC.md` were updated. `docs/DESIGN-DECISIONS.md` and `DECISIONS.md` still mention the old words as history. Leave them.
- Do not trust the test counts in `README.md`. They are older than the live site.
