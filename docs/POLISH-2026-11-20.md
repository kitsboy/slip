## What changed

- All five sections, plus Learn, now have named accessible regions and a consistent page heading / explanation / content rhythm. The current section no longer scrolls underneath the unusually tall responsive header: a ResizeObserver supplies the actual header height to hash scroll margins.
- A waiting sale is a full-width, neutral in-flow return link instead of a floating brass pill covering the current page. Its wording and destination are unchanged.
- Sell inherits readable wrapping shared controls without moving its payment bar, changing totals or adding another primary. Books gets separated checklist rows and clearer unresolved-sale dividers. Setup and More have consistent section/card spacing and disclosure targets.
- Money in groups its existing request fields under a semantic fieldset, with a named legend and more space between fields. No request calculation or persistence changed.
- Setup guidance has a tighter first-screen rhythm and a programmatic current-step marker. Large reading type now follows the shop's text setting rather than being pinned at 16px.
- Shared buttons have a 48px target and wrapping labels; other existing secondary controls keep their 44px floor. Blocked controls remain focusable and no longer fade their explanatory text. Checkbox labels, disclosures, selection and field carets have consistent treatment.
- Dialog and Sheet share focus containment, visible/enabled focusable discovery (including selects and textareas), Escape handling, opener restoration and page-scroll locking. Modal surfaces have bounded viewport height, internal scrolling and safe-area clearance.
- Customer display status is announced politely; long shop names wrap and payment panels use a content-sized responsive grid. Digital receipts get a readable narrow layout and status hierarchy. Payment status wording, URI producers, QR artwork sizing and complete receipt links are untouched.
- Screenshot matrix now actually opens the gallery (`gallery=1`, not the obsolete `parts=1`), includes More, and captures 320/390/768/1440 widths.

The incumbent dark warm v2 canvas remains in both theme selections, as required by the product lane. No new palette, dependency, font, network call, printing feature, financial/storage-engine edit or honesty-label change.
