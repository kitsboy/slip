## Do not break

Browser shift commits use `src/core/shift-store.ts`: Web Lock → authoritative IndexedDB
witness → prepared intent → guarded localStorage mirror → committed witness. Missing APIs
or an ambiguous interrupted intent reject rather than pretend to save. All shift mutations
use `mutateShift` / `replaceShiftLog` in `src/core/storage.ts`; never synchronous shift-key
writes. Await retry and demo exit. Wipe explicitly replaces the log first, not an append.
Normal reads remain localStorage. Old/noncooperating writers are outside the protocol:
close old tabs. This is not general cross-device sync or atomicity for all ledger records.

Ordinary reads do not expose unsaved recovery data. Recovery notes never auto-create a
sale. No seed/private keys, custody or dishonest payment status; no money arithmetic changes.
Shop data lives on each merchant's device, not THOR or the Pi. Source/docs are on THOR.
