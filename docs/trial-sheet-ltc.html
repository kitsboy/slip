Litecoin trial — one sitting, about ten minutes

Three clicks only a human can do, then seven steps with a shopkeeper. Nothing here needs the internet explained, and nothing here involves real money.

Three clicks

  1. Get test coins into slip's own address. Open cypherfaucet.com/ltc-testnet, paste my6RhGaMEf8v9yyQKqiuUYniJLfyU4gzqe, tick the captcha, claim 0.01 tLTC. Then open the till that owns that key and press check now. The receipt should go awaiting → seen, then confirmed after a block. If it does not, that is the finding of the year.
  2. Scan a receipt with a real camera. Charge any pretend sale, show the receipt, point a second phone's camera at the QR. It must read the address printed beside it.
  3. Open the site on the phone you will use, not a desktop, and tap every tab once.

Seven steps, no explaining allowed

AskWatch for
1. “Set up a shop that takes Litecoin testnet.”Where they look first. Do they read the note under the network picker?
2. “Paste this key. It is public.”Does the key go in without a fight?
3. “Compare address 0/0 with your wallet.”Do they really compare, or tick “They match” to get past it?
4. “Sell me a coffee for 0.0005 LTC.”The rate line, the amount pad, the Charge button.
5. “Now say I paid, but I have not.”Does the till refuse to say Paid? Does “check now” explain itself?
6. “Print the receipt.”Torn edge, QR read by a second phone.
7. “Close the day.”Do the totals mean anything to them?

Write down every question asked, word for word, and every pause longer than three seconds. The list of hesitations is the real result of the trial — “it worked” is not.
